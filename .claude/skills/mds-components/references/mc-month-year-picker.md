# mc-month-year-picker

Select month and year from two separate pickers

**Url:** https://designsystem.maersk.com/
**Alternative names:** Month Year Selector, Month Year Dropdown
**Keywords:** 
**✅ When to use:** When you need to select a month and year without a specific day
**❌ When not to use:** When you need to select a specific date
